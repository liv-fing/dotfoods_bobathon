# Stage 1 — On Your Own

Complete these steps on your own before the Bobathon. You will need a laptop with internet access.

---

## 0.1 — Pre-Requisite Bob Setup

Check your inbox for the **"You've been invited to join your team"** email, then follow these steps to install Bob and sign in for the first time.

**Step 1 — Create your IBM ID**
Open the invitation email and click the link to create your IBM ID. This gives you an account to access IBM Bob.

**Step 2 — Download & open Bob**
Download Bob using the correct installer for your computer, then open it. If Bob asks whether to import settings from another editor, click your regular IDE or **Skip for now**. If it asks about chat migration, click **Skip migration**.

**Step 3 — Log in to Bob**
In the Bob chat window, click **Log in to Bob**. When asked to allow Bob to sign in, click **Allow**. Bob will then ask if you want to open a link — click **Open**. This opens your browser to complete the sign-in.

**Step 4 — Verify your account is active**
Click the **gear icon** above the Bob chat window. Under the **General** tab, confirm that coins are listed in your budget at **100% remaining** — this means your account is active and ready.

![Bob settings panel showing the gear icon and General settings](img/settings-pt1.png)

> 💡 If you belong to multiple teams, a team dropdown will appear — make sure it shows a team with **Bobathon** in the name.

![Bob team selector showing Bobathon account option](img/change-team.png)

## 0.2 — Verify and Install Required Extensions

Two extensions are required. The **IBM Bob Premium Package for i** is often pre-installed for your team — verify it below. The **IBM i Development Pack** must be installed manually.

**Step 1 — Open the Extensions view**
Click the Extensions icon in the Activity Bar on the far left of Bob, or press `Cmd+Shift+X` (macOS) / `Ctrl+Shift+X` (Windows/Linux).

![Extensions icon in the Bob Activity Bar](img/open-market.png)

**Step 2 — Verify IBM Bob Premium Package for i**
Search for `"IBM Bob Premium"`. If **IBM Bob Premium Package for i** (publisher: IBM) appears with an **Install** button, click **Install**. If there is no Install button, you're all set — skip to step 3.

![Searching for IBM Bob Premium in the Extensions panel](img/searching-ibm-i.png)

**Step 3 — Install IBM i Development Pack**
Search for `"IBM i Development Pack"` in the Extensions panel. Click **Install** if it does not appear under *Installed*. Reload Bob if prompted.

**Step 4 — Open Bob Settings**
Look towards the bottom-right corner of the Bob IDE and click the **Bob Settings** button to open the Settings panel.

![Bob Settings button in the bottom-right corner of the Bob IDE](img/bob_settings.png)

**Step 5 — Install Premium Package for i**
In the Settings panel, find the **Add ons** section. Click **Install** next to IBM Bob Premium Package for i. Restart Bob if prompted.

![IBM Bob Premium Package for i shown next to the Add ons setting with an Install button](img/activatedd.png)

**Step 6 — Confirm it is activated**
Return to the Bob chat by clicking the icon in the top-right corner. Click **Agent** or the **+** sign — you should see **IBM i Developer** and **IBM i Database** listed as mode options. If they don't appear, your account may not yet be linked to your Premium Package for i entitlements.

![Icon in the top-right corner of Bob to return to the chat](img/sidebar-button.png)

![IBM i Developer and IBM i Database modes in the mode selector](img/new-modes.png)
